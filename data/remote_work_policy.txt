PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT
This editable template provides a structured starting point for a remote
and hybrid work policy. Customize bracketed fields and validate legal
requirements before adoption.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- This policy sets out how employees may work from home or another
  approved location, so that flexibility is offered fairly while
  productivity, security, collaboration and employee well-being are
  maintained.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- This draft applies to employees of [Organization Name]. The
  organization should define whether it covers permanent, probationary,
  fixed-term, part-time, contract and intern roles.
- Remote work is a work arrangement, not a separate employment
  contract. Applicable employment laws, state rules, standing orders
  and employment contracts take precedence over this template.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. POLICY PRINCIPLES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Remote work is a privilege tied to role suitability and business
  needs; it is not an automatic entitlement.
- Remote employees remain bound by all organizational policies,
  including the Code of Conduct, the Leave Policy and the POSH policy.
- Expectations for output, availability and communication are the same
  whether an employee works on-site or remotely.
- Arrangements should be applied consistently and without
  discrimination.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WORK ARRANGEMENT TYPES - PROPOSED FRAMEWORK
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- On-site: Works from the office or designated work location on all
  working days.
- Hybrid: Works from the office on [Insert Number] days per week and
  remotely on the remaining days. Suggested planning assumption: 2-3
  office days per week.
- Fully remote: Works remotely on all working days. Suitable only for
  roles approved as remote by the manager and HR.
- Temporary remote work: Short-term arrangement for reasons such as
  illness recovery, travel disruption, extreme weather or personal
  circumstances, with a defined end date.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ELIGIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Role suitability: The job duties can be performed effectively
  without a physical presence.
- Performance: The employee is meeting expectations. New joiners and
  employees on a performance plan may be asked to work on-site for an
  initial period of [Insert Period].
- Readiness: The employee has a suitable workspace, reliable internet
  and the equipment needed to work securely.
- The numbers above are illustrative planning assumptions only. HR/legal
  should confirm requirements for each work location and employee
  category.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
6. REQUEST AND APPROVAL PROCESS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Request: Submit a request through the approved HR system or form,
  stating the proposed arrangement, work location and duration.
- Approval: The reporting manager reviews role suitability and team
  coverage. HR reviews policy, legal and security questions.
- Review: Approved arrangements are reviewed every [Insert Period], and
  may be changed or withdrawn with reasonable notice for business or
  performance reasons.
- Ad hoc remote days: Notify the manager in advance through the
  approved channel, and obtain approval where the policy requires it.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WORKING HOURS, AVAILABILITY AND COMMUNICATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Employees should be reachable during agreed core hours of
  [Insert Core Hours] through the approved communication tools.
- Attendance and working hours should be recorded through the approved
  system. Overtime must be pre-approved and handled according to
  applicable law and the employment contract.
- Employees should attend scheduled meetings, respond to messages
  within a reasonable time and update their calendar or status when
  unavailable.
- Planned absence is recorded as leave under the Leave Policy. Working
  remotely is not a substitute for leave.
- Managers should respect personal time and avoid routine requests
  outside agreed working hours, except for genuine urgent needs.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
8. WORKSPACE, EQUIPMENT AND EXPENSES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Employees are responsible for a safe, quiet and reasonably private
  workspace with a dependable internet connection.
- Organization-issued equipment remains company property, must be used
  for work purposes, and must be returned when the arrangement or
  employment ends.
- Equipment, internet or utility support: [Insert Policy - e.g.
  Provided / Reimbursed up to INR [Amount] per month / Not provided].
  Expense claims must follow the approved reimbursement process.
- Loss, theft or damage of equipment must be reported to IT and the
  manager immediately.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
9. INFORMATION SECURITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Use only approved devices, accounts, VPN and collaboration tools for
  work. Do not store company data on personal accounts or unapproved
  storage.
- Lock devices when unattended, use strong passwords and multi-factor
  authentication, and keep software up to date.
- Avoid working on confidential matters in public places or on public
  Wi-Fi without the approved security controls.
- Do not allow family members or others to access company devices or
  data.
- Suspected security incidents or data breaches must be reported to IT
  or the designated security contact immediately.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
10. HEALTH, SAFETY AND WELL-BEING
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Employees should take regular breaks, set up an ergonomic workspace
  and raise any work-related health or safety concerns with their
  manager or HR.
- Work-related injuries should be reported promptly. HR will advise on
  next steps in line with applicable law.
- Employees may ask HR about reasonable adjustments, and requests will
  be handled confidentially.
- Workplace conduct standards, including the POSH policy, continue to
  apply to video calls, chats and other virtual interactions.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
11. WORKING FROM OUTSIDE THE USUAL LOCATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Employees must work from the location approved in their arrangement.
  Working from another city, state or country requires prior written
  approval from the manager and HR.
- Cross-border work may have tax, immigration, data protection and
  social security implications and is not permitted without HR/legal
  review.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
12. PERFORMANCE AND ACCOUNTABILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Performance is assessed on outcomes, quality, timeliness and
  collaboration, not on visible activity alone.
- Managers should set clear goals and hold regular check-ins.
- Employees should flag blockers early and keep their work visible
  through agreed tools and updates.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
13. MISUSE, EXCEPTIONS AND DISPUTES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Misreporting working hours, breaching security rules or repeated
  failure to follow this policy may lead to withdrawal of the remote
  arrangement and may be handled under the organization's disciplinary
  process, consistent with applicable law.
- Exceptions should be documented and approved by HR or the designated
  authority. Employees may raise questions or concerns with HR without
  retaliation.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
14. RECORDS AND PRIVACY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Remote work approvals, attendance and equipment records are kept by
  HR/IT and accessible only to people who need them for HR,
  management, compliance or payroll purposes.
- Monitoring of devices or activity, if any, must be proportionate,
  disclosed in advance and consistent with applicable data protection
  law.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
15. REVIEW AND GOVERNANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Policy owner: HR / People Operations.
- Approver: [Authorized Approver / Leadership Team].
- Review frequency: Annually, and whenever applicable law or
  organizational requirements change.
